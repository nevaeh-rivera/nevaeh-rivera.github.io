My connection to Hawai'i

Aloha, My name is Nevaeh and I am born and raised in Hawai'i. Growing up, Ived always enjoying the scenery and looking out the window. I feel like Hawai'i is my home and the only place where I want to be.There is no other place I want to be. Something of hawai'i thats a big part of me is the ocean. I love ging swimming, paddling and relaxing when I have free time. I also like to work outside and learn form the 'aina. I believe that learning shouldnt come from 4 walls of a classroom with paper , tests and pencils. I believe that we shoul have connections with 'aina and our kupuna so we can learn from them and get the education our community needs and deserves.

Purpose of this site

The purpose of this site is to bring awareness to Hawaiian Practices. I want to bring awarenss because I once didnt know my why we sing Hawai'i Pono'i, I once wasnt aware of my culture but would see daily things. This is only a awareness page so I dont know everything, but what I can offer is my 'ike and what i learned. The purpse of this website is to let visitors have access to Hawaiian cultrual websites and not just text books. Also to learn the "basics" and what I think is important for people to learn.

Why does it matter?

This matters because it helps restore Hawai'i, helping loclas, native hawaiians and the community come as one. By participating in these practices, you are uplifing the culture. Even just by learning a simple word and sharing it is helping. This matters to me because I never thought about why they oli to enter a space, or why its so important. It matters because we were once banned from Hawaiian Culture.This is the time of restoring and helping Hawai'i be Hawai'i again.
