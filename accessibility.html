Accesibility Statment

Commitment

On my website, Restoration of Land and culture: Hawai'i, I believe that the accessibility of learning Hawaiian culture and Practices should be available to everyone. I am ensuring that this website is accessible with visitors of all abilities. I am committed that I made a website for Hawaiian culture and the needs of the community.

Accessibility Features

To provide the best quality and experience for all users, This site includes these features:

  • Elements like html, main, header and nav to help readers navigate through my pages better.
  • Alt text: images include a alt text to describe visual content to users if they cant see them.
  • Design: My pages are designed for readers and visitors to look at one section at a time, the boxes I create are made for my content to stand out and so its readabile across all devices.
  • Clear contrast:My text and backround colors are made for visual and easy to see and read.

Testing

For the accessibility of my website I tested it with these following:

  • WAVE: to identify my structual errors
  • Visiual Testing: Reviewing contrast, heading hiearchy for the best visuals and readabile content.
